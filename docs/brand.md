# Barktrace brand

![Barktrace lockup](assets/barktrace-lockup.svg)

## Identity

**Name:** Barktrace

**Descriptor:** Lean, self-hosted error tracking

**Tagline:** Follow every failure.

**Positioning:** Error tracking for teams that want Sentry SDK compatibility,
SSO, and operational ownership without a large infrastructure footprint.

The name joins a dog's immediate signal—the bark—with the engineering act of
following a failure through its trace. The canine reference should remain
subtle: vigilant and dependable, never cartoonish.

## Voice

- Direct: say what failed and what the user can do next.
- Calm: incidents are urgent enough without dramatic interface language.
- Technical: use precise event, issue, release, and trace terminology.
- Independent: emphasize self-hosting and ownership without attacking hosted
  alternatives.

Examples:

- “3 new issues since the latest release.”
- “No events received yet. Send a test event with this DSN.”
- “Follow every failure.”

Avoid dog puns in product actions and error messages. Barktrace is the name,
not a mascot-driven vocabulary.

## Visual system

The mark combines an abstract hound silhouette with a signal trace. It must be
shown upright and should not be redrawn as a detailed dog illustration.

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal | Acid green | `#c5f11e` |
| Signal on light | Leaf green | `#9bd319` |
| Ink | Near black | `#171916` |
| Night background | Deep charcoal | `#111310` |
| Paper background | Soft white | `#fafafa` |
| Incident | Warm red | `#dc503d` |

Use lowercase `barktrace` for the visual wordmark and title case `Barktrace` in
prose. The preferred logotype is a heavy geometric sans-serif with tight
tracking. Product UI remains compact, neutral, and information-dense.
